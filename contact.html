What to write about

A mistake in the app
A wrong answer key, a confusing explanation, a bad translation or a typo. These are the most useful messages to send. Please quote the question or the screen so it can be found.
Something that does not work
Say what you did, what happened, and what you expected. Your Android version and phone model help, and so does the app version — it is at the bottom of the About screen in Settings.
Privacy
Any question about the Privacy Policy. Note that the app keeps your data only on your own phone, so there is no account for us to look up, export or delete on your behalf — the app's own Backup and Delete all data screens do both directly.
Purchases and refunds
The paid upgrade is sold and billed by Google Play, so refunds, cancellations and payment problems must go through Google Play rather than through us. Write anyway if a feature you paid for is not unlocking, and use Restore Purchases in the app first.
Translations
The app is translated into eleven languages, and the translations other than English are machine-drafted and awaiting review by native speakers. Corrections from a fluent speaker are very welcome.

What we cannot help with

We are not connected to the military or to the ASVAB in any way, so we cannot answer questions about registering for the real test, your official scores, waivers, recruiting, enlistment or eligibility for a particular job. A recruiter or an official Military Entrance Processing Station is the place for those.

Mailing address

No postal address is published for this independently developed app. If you need one for a formal or legal notice, ask by e-mail and it will be provided.
