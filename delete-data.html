Delete your data

Last updated 5 October 2026

This page is for ASVAB Prep 2026, the Android app published on Google Play by Zainab Zia. There is no account and no sign-in, so almost everything the app holds is already on your own phone and you can erase it yourself in a few seconds. A small amount of usage, crash and purchase data is held by Google and by RevenueCat on the app's behalf; you can ask for that to be deleted too, and the steps are below.

Step 1 — erase everything on your phone

Everything you study is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. To erase it:

  1. Open ASVAB Prep 2026.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete all data.
  4. Confirm. The deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Uninstalling the app removes the same data. Either way nothing is kept on your phone afterwards, and there is no copy of it anywhere else — your answers, scores and notes are never transmitted.

Step 2 — request deletion of the data held off your phone

To ask for the usage, crash and purchase records described below to be deleted:

  1. E-mail zainabzia571@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request.
  2. Say that your request is for ASVAB Prep 2026 on Android.
  3. If you bought the upgrade, include the Google Play order number from your Play receipt. That is the only way a purchase record can be found, because the app holds no name or e-mail address for you.
  4. If you did not buy anything, say so. There is then no identifier that can be traced back to you, and the reply will explain what can and cannot be found — see the note at the end of this page.

Requests are acted on within 30 days of being received, and you will get a reply confirming what was deleted.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted

  • Everything on your phone — answers, scores, progress, the mistake bank, bookmarks, notes, scratch paper, flashcard history, study plan, reminders and settings. Erased by Step 1, immediately, by you. Nothing is retained.
  • Your purchase record at RevenueCat — the purchase Google reported and the installation identifier it was recorded against. Deleted on request, if you supply the Play order number.
  • Crash reports — the technical details of any crash of the app on your device. Deleted on request where they can still be identified, and otherwise deleted automatically by Google after 90 days.
  • Usage events — the ten events listed in the Privacy Policy. Deleted on request where they can still be identified, and otherwise deleted automatically by Google 14 months after they were recorded.

Kept, and why

  • Your Google Play purchase and billing history. Google Play is the seller, not this app, and Google keeps its own transaction records for tax and accounting purposes for as long as the law requires. This app cannot delete them. Google's privacy policy governs that data.
  • Aggregate counts. Figures such as how many people opened the app in a given week survive deletion, because once combined they no longer relate to any individual and cannot be separated back out.
  • Nothing else. There is no account, no profile and no mailing list, so there is nothing else to retain.

If you never bought the upgrade, there may be nothing to delete. Usage and crash data is recorded against an identifier that Google generates for your installation of the app. It is not your name, your e-mail address or your Google account, and nobody — including the developer — can work out from an e-mail which identifier is yours. Uninstalling the app ends that identifier, and the data expires on the schedule above. Write anyway and you will get an honest answer about what could be found.
