Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

The short version. ASVAB Prep 2026 has no account, no sign-in and no server. Everything you do in the app is written to the app's own storage on your phone and stays there. The published app does not even ask Android for internet access, so it cannot send your study data anywhere.

Introduction

This policy explains what ASVAB Prep 2026 ("the app") does with information when you use it on your Android device. It is written for the app as published on Google Play, and it is the same text that is bundled inside the app so you can read it offline.

The app is made by an independent developer. Where this policy says "we", it means that developer, reachable at zainabzia571@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

We do not collect anything. There is no account to create, nothing to log in to, and no server that belongs to the app. No personal information is gathered, transmitted, sold, shared or profiled, and there is no advertising, analytics or tracking software in the app.

The app does store information on your device so that it can work. That data is described next. It is yours, it never leaves the phone unless you export it yourself, and we have no way to see it.

Study Data

To show your progress, the app keeps the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • your answers, the quizzes and practice tests you take, and the scores
  • which lessons you have read and your flashcard progress
  • bookmarked questions, the mistakes you are still working on, and scratch notes taken during a quiz
  • your study streak, daily goal, chosen test date, and the branch of service you said you were aiming for
  • your settings: language, theme, text size, reminders
  • quizzes you have created or opened from a shared code

None of this is personally identifying and none of it is uploaded. The app never asks for your name, date of birth, address, phone number, e-mail address, contacts, location or any government identifier.

Device Information

The app reads a small amount of information from the device in order to display itself correctly, and only while it is running:

  • the system language and region, so it can start in your language and format dates and numbers the way you write them
  • the system's dark-mode setting, wallpaper colour palette and text size, so the app can follow them
  • the Android version, so features that exist only on newer releases can be turned on or off

This information is used on the spot and is not recorded, catalogued or sent anywhere. The app does not read any device or advertising identifier, and it does not fingerprint your device.

Permissions

The app asks for as little as it can get away with:

Notifications
Only so that a study reminder you set yourself can appear at the time you chose. If you deny it, the rest of the app works and the app tells you plainly that reminders will not arrive.
Run after restart
So that reminders you have already set survive the phone being switched off and on again. Nothing else happens at start-up.
Camera (optional)
Only used on the screen where you scan a shared-quiz code, and only while that screen is open. The camera image is examined on the device to read the code; no photo is taken, kept or transmitted. The app declares the camera as optional hardware, so it installs on devices without one, and you can refuse the permission and still use everything else.
Internet
Not requested. The published app does not hold the Android internet permission, which is why its questions, lessons and practice tests are all bundled in the download. If you tap a link to open this website, Android hands that address to your own browser — the app itself still makes no connection.
Storage
Not requested. When you save or restore a backup, the app uses Android's own file picker, so you choose the file or folder and the app is given access to that one item only.

Data Storage

Everything is stored in the app's private area on your device, which other apps cannot read. It is not encrypted beyond the encryption Android already applies to the device as a whole, because it contains no sensitive personal information — only your practice history.

There is no retention period, because there is nothing held anywhere else. Your data lives as long as the app is installed. Uninstalling the app deletes it. The app also has a Delete all data screen that clears everything it has saved in one step, and tells you exactly what will go.

Backup & Restore

The app can write everything it has saved into a single backup file, and read that file back later. This is entirely under your control:

  • you choose where the file goes, through Android's file picker
  • the file is plain, readable JSON, and it is not uploaded anywhere by the app
  • where the file ends up afterwards is up to you. If you save it to a cloud folder or send it to yourself, that service's own privacy policy applies to it, not this one
  • restoring replaces what is currently in the app, and the app warns you before it does

A purchase is deliberately not in the backup file, because it belongs to your Google account rather than to this data.

Third Party Services

The app contains no advertising network, no analytics package and no crash-reporting service. Three third parties can still be involved, and only in these narrow ways:

Google Play
Handles the optional paid upgrade. All payment details are given to Google, never to the app: we never see your card, billing address or Google account. The app stores only whether the upgrade is currently active, so it keeps working offline. Google's handling of the transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
Your device's text-to-speech engine
If you ask the app to read a lesson or question aloud, that text is handed to whichever speech engine is installed on your phone — often Google's. Depending on the engine and its settings, it may process the text on the device or on its own servers. Nothing about you is included, only the study text being read, and the feature does nothing until you press play.
Your browser
If you open this website from inside the app, Android passes the address to the browser you have chosen, which then applies its own policy.

Security

Because nothing is transmitted and nothing is stored on a server, there is no account to be breached and no database of users to leak. Your data is protected by Android's own app sandbox and by the device-level encryption your phone applies when it is locked.

The security of anything you export is in your hands: a backup file you have saved or shared is an ordinary file, readable by whoever has it.

User Rights

Rights such as access, correction, portability, erasure and objection normally mean asking a company for a copy of what it holds about you. Here they are already satisfied by the app itself, without asking anyone:

  • Access and portability — Backup writes everything the app holds into a file you keep, in a readable format.
  • Correction — every setting can be changed, and progress can be reset from within the app.
  • Erasure — Delete all data, or uninstalling, removes everything.
  • Objection and restriction — there is no processing to object to: no profiling, no automated decisions, no marketing, no transfer to anyone.

We cannot action a request about your data even if you send one, because we do not hold it.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for people preparing for military entrance testing, which generally means ages 17 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone of any age — there is nothing to collect. There is no chat, no user-generated content shown to other people, no social feature and no advertising, so a younger user cannot be contacted or profiled through the app.

Changes to Policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published on this page with a new date at the top, and the copy bundled inside the app will be updated in the next release. A change that would newly involve collecting or transmitting data would be announced in the app itself before it took effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in the app, can be sent to zainabzia571@gmail.com. Please allow a few days for a reply; this is an independently developed app, not a company with a support desk.
