Legal information

Last updated 5 October 2026

ASVAB Prep 2026 is an Android study app for the Armed Services Vocational Aptitude Battery. It runs entirely on your phone.

There is no account and no sign-in. Nothing you study — your answers, scores, streaks, notes or settings — is sent anywhere. It all stays in the app's own storage on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it. The app connects to the internet for three things, none of which involve what you study: checking with Google Play whether you bought the optional upgrade, counting how the app is used, and reporting its own crashes.

These pages are published so that the policy shown inside the app can also be read on the web, which Google Play requires. The same text is bundled in the app itself, so you can read it with no connection.

About the app

ASVAB Prep 2026 bundles its questions, lessons, flashcards and practice tests inside the download, so every feature works with the phone in aeroplane mode. Some features are part of a paid upgrade, which is sold only through Google Play.
